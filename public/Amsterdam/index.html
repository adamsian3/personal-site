Amsterdam: a relaxed city with culture on every canal

1 March 2016Netherlands, AmsterdamVisited Jun 2024, Apr 2016

Key takeaways

  • Go for: a relaxed European city with plenty of culture and history
  • Don't miss: the Anne Frank House, for the history and the stories
  • Best coffee: Coffee Company, the only 5/5 of the trip
  • Skip: the Hard Rock Cafe. The food was average and there are better places to eat
  • Pro tip: plan your route before you set off. The canals make it easy to end up going in circles
At a glance
Best for Culture, history, a weekend away
Time needed 2 days
Budget ££
Best time to go Summer (June or July), when it's warmest
Getting around Mostly walking. Staying longer? Hire a bike to explore further

The brief

Two trips, eight years apart. The first was a weekend in April 2016 with a partner. I went back in June 2024 for a work trip.

Top placements

1. Anne Frank House


The canal house where Anne Frank and her family hid from the Nazis in a secret annex during the Second World War. Go for the history and the stories. Map

2. Heineken Experience


The brewery tour in the old Heineken brewery. Map

3. Royal Palace


Used for royal receptions since 1808, right on Dam Square. Map

  • Tip: do the tour inside. We didn't, and I wish we had.

4. The Bulldog

A very cool bar. It sells space cakes, if you want to try them. Map

5. Sexmuseum


One for a laugh. Map

Also seen: the Waag, a medieval city gate turned weighing house, the National Monument on Dam Square, and the view from SkyLounge Amsterdam.

Connected

  • Apps that helped: Google Maps and Citymapper both work well here

Where I stayed

  • The Student Hotel: recommended. It's a short trip into town and the breakfast was good.

Good to know

  • Amsterdam has over 400 km of bike lanes, and around 25,000 bikes end up in the canals every year.
  • It began as a small fishing village at the mouth of the Amstel in the 12th century, and became a major world port in the Dutch Golden Age.
  • The Waag square was created in 1614, when the canals around it were filled in, and was used as a market.

The verdict

Would I rebook? Yes. Next time I want to see more of the museums and take a boat tour of the docks.

Next time

  • See: more museums, starting with the Van Gogh Museum, a boat tour of the docks, Vondelpark, Noordermarkt and the Amsterdam Wine Festival
  • Eat and drink: TOZI Restaurant & Bar, Hannekes Boom, SAINT-JEAN pastry shop and Uncommon (café)
