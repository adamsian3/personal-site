March 2026: Weddings, AI Festivals and Crimson Desert

31 March 2026

What's been going on

March was a busy one. A lot happening on the work side with big Spotify and reMarkable deliverables landing at the same time, while personally the wedding planning has well and truly kicked off. Attended two wedding shows, gave formal notice of marriage, and somehow still found time to sink hours into a new game. Classic.

Work & Industry

  • AI Festival 2026, The Human Side of AI Adoption, Attended a session mid-month on how agencies are actually integrating AI into day-to-day workflows. The framing around adoption being a people problem more than a tech problem rang true. Most teams have the tools, not everyone has the habits.

  • Wispr Flow, Started using this voice-to-text tool properly on a WFH day when I wasn't feeling 100%. Turned out to be one of my most productive writing days in a while. If you do a lot of email or note-taking, worth a try, it gets out of your own way fast.

Tech & Gadgets

  • Colorize Image Tool, Found pinetools.com/colorize-image while working on some creative assets. Simple but genuinely useful if you need to add colour to black and white images quickly.

  • Wispr Flow, Mentioned above in work context but worth flagging here too as a personal productivity tool. Voice-first note taking is underrated.
