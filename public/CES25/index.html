My CES 2025 Recap

11 January 2025

NVIDIA

  • New RTX 50 series of Graphics Cards Using Blackwell architecture. They quote the lowest costing graphics card 4070 will be as powerful as the last series top card 4090 with frame generation AI tech. Link
  • These cards are also smaller then last gen, meaning they take less space up in peoples computers.
  • They will require more power, so might be hotter quicker. though testing will be needed to check how they perform in these smaller shells

AMD

  • New Ryzen 9 processors including the 9950X3D. CPU focused on gaming with 16 cores. this will be the best mainstream CPU for gamers in the world.

SteamOS

  • Steam are now lettings 3rd party hardware providers like Lenovo's Legion Go S to make handheld gamign consoles. To date only Steam had released their own device called Steamdeck.
  • This should help keep high market share on the handheld PC gaming market.

Smart glasses

  • Startup Halliday will make you the smartest person in the room with their new invisible display glasses. These sleek specs, designed to compete with Meta Ray-Bans (on function and price), have a built-in display that syncs with an AI assistant. You talk, it listens and anticipates. Questions? Answers at the ready. Need meeting notes? Captured.

Roomba with Arm

  • Roborock’s vacuum cleaner doesn’t just suck up dust bunnies, it tackles unexpected obstacles with a robotic arm. No more need to intervene and pick up socks for this little whirring wonder. 

Self charging pool cleaner

  • WYBOT S2 Solar Vision’s pool cleaning robot autonomously returns to its solar-powered underwater charging station after completing an entire cleaning cycle.

Emotional Intellect Bots

  • Groundbreakers like Mirumi, and Mirokai are showing us that bots can have feelings too! Well, sort of... Designed for emotional interactions and elderly care respectively, these bots might change our perception of companionship and healthcare.

Expandable laptop screen

  • Lenovo’s ThinkBook Plus Gen 6 14-inch productivity laptop, albeit with a slightly square-ish aspect ratio and a resolution of 2000 x 1600. But hitting a dedicated key or raising your hand to the webcam activates the motors and makes the display climb to a tall 16.7 inches, with an extended resolution of 2000 x 2350.
    • Looks like crap but if more devices start to use this tech it will mean phones, iPads will change in size to adapt to a users need.

The MCON by Ohsnap is a portable mobile gaming controller.

  • Magnetic Attachment: MCON magnetically attaches to the back of your phone, making it easy to use and store.
  • Bluetooth Connectivity: Provides a hassle-free, low-latency gaming experience without finicky connections.
  • Customizable Fit: Includes two pucks and a MagSafe adapter for a tailored fit, ensuring compatibility with various phone models and cases.

The new Alienware monitor

  • 4K QD-OLED Gaming Monitor (AW2725Q) 27inch DCI-P3 99% colour coverage and Delta E<2 color accuracy. 240Hz refresh rate and 0.03ms response time.
    • Flat panel vs the curved one they released last year.
  • $899.99 (US) - EMEA in April, 2025.
