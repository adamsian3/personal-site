#TVTwitter - How advertisers get closer to the conversation

31 March 2014

  1. TV and Twitter Integration: Research conducted by Thinkbox, Twitter, and Brain Juicer highlights the positive relationship between TV and Twitter. TV drives word-of-mouth conversations, and TV advertising contributes to brand conversations on social media.
  2. Viewer Engagement: Qualitative research reveals that viewers have two main modes of engagement: "lean back" for relaxation and comfort, and "lean forward" for active and connection-based viewing. TV advertising should focus on the context of the moment and setting.
  3. Second Screen Behavior: Viewers today often divide their attention between the TV screen and a second screen (e.g., mobile device). This behavior, known as "meriting," presents opportunities for brands to engage with viewers through digital calls to action.
  4. Twitter's Impact: Twitter advertising has positive implications for brands, as it keeps viewers engaged and ready to interact with content through hashtags or URLs. Emotional reactions and key moments in TV shows can generate spikes in Twitter activity, which can be planned for and utilized.
  5. Elements of TV-Supported Content: Successful integration of TV and Twitter requires a total understanding of brands across both mediums, anticipation of opportunities within content, and the creation of unique experiences that resonate with viewers. Case studies demonstrate the effectiveness of this understanding in driving sales.

Note: The original text is from 2014, so it's important to consider that the landscape and dynamics may have evolved since then.
