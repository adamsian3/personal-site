Dubrovnik: The Old Town, Lokrum and a birthday

12 November 2026Croatia, DubrovnikVisited Oct 2016

Key takeaways

  • Go for: the Old Town
  • Don't miss: Lokrum Island
  • Skip: kayaking, a waste of time
  • Pro tip: book Game of Thrones and movie scene tours before you go, and read the reviews first
At a glance
Best for The Old Town
Time needed 3 to 4 days
Budget ££

The brief

A birthday holiday with my partner and two friends, 15 to 21 October 2016.

Top placements

1. Lokrum Island


The boat trip to Lokrum is worth it, as the island is so nice for a day trip and picnic. We saw the peacocks and rabbits. Map

Worth going up to the top of the hill so you can see the whole city and area. Map
