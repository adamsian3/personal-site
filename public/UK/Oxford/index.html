Oxford: dreaming spires, museums and a pub full of ties

5 January 2023UK, OxfordVisited Dec 2022, Jul 2019

Key takeaways

  • Go for: cobbled streets, historic colleges and world-class museums, an easy train ride from London
  • Don't miss: the Ashmolean Museum
  • Best coffee: Jericho Coffee Traders, 5/5
  • Skip: Victors Oxford. It's full of content creators, so find somewhere more authentic and Oxford-feeling
  • Pro tip: plan what you want to do before you go, and book Oxford Castle & Prison ahead to get the slot you want

The brief

Two trips by train from London with my partner, in July 2019 and December 2022.

Top placements

1. The Ashmolean Museum


Britain's first public museum, opened in 1683, with art and archaeology from around the world: Egyptian mummies, Greek and Roman sculpture, and drawings by Michelangelo and Raphael. Map

2. Oxford Castle & Prison


A Norman castle from 1071 that spent most of its life as a prison. The guided tour takes you through the old cells and up the Saxon tower. Map

  • Tip: book ahead to get the slot you want.

3. The Bodleian Library


The university's main research library and one of the oldest in Europe, holding over 13 million printed items. Map

  • Tip: the Bridge of Sighs is a two-minute walk away. It links two parts of Hertford College and is named after the one in Venice. Map

4. The Bear


One of Oxford's oldest pubs, best known for its collection of ties, dating back to the early 1900s, from clubs around Oxford and, more recently, around the world. Map

5. The Covered Market


An indoor market that's been trading since 1774, with stalls selling everything from fresh produce to handmade crafts. A good spot for souvenirs and gifts. Map

Also seen: the University of Oxford's colleges, each with its own history and architecture, including Magdalen with its gardens and deer park Map; and the Oxford Botanic Garden, a peaceful spot in the middle of the city Map.

Good to know

  • The University of Oxford is the oldest in the English-speaking world, with teaching there from 1096.
  • The Oxford Botanic Garden, founded in 1621, is the oldest botanic garden in Britain.
  • Christ Church was used as a filming location for the Harry Potter films.
