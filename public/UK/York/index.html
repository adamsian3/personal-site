York: history on every street

26 September 2021UK, YorkVisited Apr 2019

Key takeaways

  • Go for: history on every street
  • Don't miss: York Minster
  • Best coffee: Gatehouse Coffee, 5/5
  • Skip: the Yorkshire Museum. There are better museums elsewhere
  • Pro tip: go for the York Beer & Cider Festival, Yorkshire's largest beer festival, every September

The brief

A trip with my partner in April 2019.

Top placements

1. York Minster


The Cathedral and Metropolitical Church of Saint Peter, completed in 1472 after several centuries of building. The first recorded church on the site was a wooden structure built in 627, and some of the stained glass dates back to the 12th century. Website · Map

2. The Shambles


An old street, parts of it dating back to the 14th century. It was once known as The Great Flesh Shambles, from the shelves butchers used to display their meat. In 1885 there were thirty-one butchers' shops on the street, and now none remain. It's said to be the inspiration for Diagon Alley in Harry Potter. Map

3. York's Chocolate Story


Opened in March 2012, it shows the history of chocolate making in York, including the Rowntree's factory, which opened in 1890. In 2018 it won gold for Guided Tour of the Year at the VisitEngland Awards. Website · Map

4. City Walls


3.4 km (2 miles) of surviving masonry, built mainly in the 13th century. Map

  • Tip: a walk round the walls takes about 2 hours.

5. Clifford's Tower


Built originally on the orders of William I to dominate the former Viking city of Jórvík. York Castle was used as a jail and prison until 1929. Map

Also seen:

  • Monk Bar, the largest and most ornate of the bars, from the early 14th century, with the city's only working portcullis (in use until 1970)
  • St. Mary's Abbey, a ruined Benedictine abbey, first founded in 1055
  • The Ghost Bus Tours, a sightseeing bus tour with stories about York's key buildings and areas
  • Yorkshire Museum, opened in 1830, in the Museum Gardens

Good to know

  • The city was founded as Eboracum in 71 AD.
  • York is home to KitKat and the Yorkie bar, and Terry's Chocolate Orange was made here until the factory closed in 2005.
  • York ham is traditionally served with Madeira sauce (see my Madeira post).

The verdict

Would I rebook? Yes, for the beer festival.
Return on investment: 9/10
