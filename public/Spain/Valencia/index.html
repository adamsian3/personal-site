Valencia: The new town and the old town

12 November 2026Spain, ValenciaVisited Apr 2025, Apr 2024, Dec 2018

Key takeaways

  • Go for: the City of Arts and Sciences
  • Don't miss: the Hemisfèric
  • Best coffee: still looking. A lot of bitter, dry coffee
  • Skip: the beach. Busy and far from the centre
  • Pro tip: buy a combined City of Arts and Sciences ticket
