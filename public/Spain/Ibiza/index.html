Ibiza: daytime edition

22 March 2022Spain, IbizaVisited Apr 2022

Key takeaways

  • Go for: a relaxing scenic trip off season, or high energy in midsummer when it's party season
  • Don't miss: the Castle of Ibiza
  • Best coffee: Melbourne Street Coffee, 4/5
  • Skip: Las Puertas de Can Soleil. Not much to see
  • Pro tip: hire a car
At a glance
Best for A family holiday
Time needed 4 to 5 days
Budget ££
Best time to go Off season if you want a relaxing scenic trip, but if you want high energy go midsummer when it's party season
Getting around Hire a car

The brief

A family holiday in April 2022.

Top placements

1. Castle of Ibiza (Castell d'Eivissa)


Map

2. Mirador des Vedrà


A popular spot to view Mediterranean sunsets, and a scenic viewing point to look over Es Vedrà island. Map

3. Cala Llentia


Thirteen basalt columns designed by the artist Andrew Rogers and paid for by Guy Laliberté. Made in 2014, it was carried out without permission, and the City of San José opened a file of urban infringement at the time. Website · Map

4. Cova de Can Marçà


Guided tours of underground caverns used by pirate smugglers, with a 10 metre cascade waterfall light show. Website · Map

Also seen: Harinus Forn Artesà, a bakery Website · Map; and Las Puertas de Can Soleil, doors with Arabic engravings by an unknown author. From the right angle and with the doors open, you can see Es Vedrà on the horizon. Map

Good to know

  • Ibiza is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, awarded in 1999.
  • Its highest point, Sa Talaiassa, is 475 metres above sea level.
  • The typical folk dance of Ibiza is Ball Pagès (Video). Its origin is unknown.
