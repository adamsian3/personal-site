Gibraltar: a mini UK away from the UK, with better weather

26 September 2021GibraltarVisited Sep 2021

Key takeaways

  • Go for: a mini UK away from the UK, with better weather
  • Don't miss: the Barbary macaques on the Upper Rock
  • Best coffee: Chök, 4/5
  • Skip: the City Under Siege Exhibition
  • Pro tip: research the jeep tours online before you go. The monkeys know the guides and will happily jump on you on command, and the guides know a lot of facts
At a glance
Best for History, views, a weekend away
Time needed 2 days
Budget ££
Best time to go Mid-summer, when the flowers and gardens are at their best
Getting around We drove, as family live a couple of hours away. Plenty of people fly in for the weekend

The brief

A family weekend away in September 2021.

Top placements

1. The Barbary macaques

A tailless species originally from the Atlas and Rif mountains of Morocco. They were once under military care: in 1944 the War Office budgeted £4 a month for their fruit, vegetables and nuts. Map

  • Tip: see them on a jeep tour. They know the guides and will jump on you on command.

2. The Rock and the Skywalk


The Rock rises 426 m, and the Skywalk viewing platform is the spot for the view. Map

3. St Michael's Cave


Limestone caves full of stalactites and stalagmites, lit up in colour and still used for concerts. The Victorians held picnics, weddings and even duels here. Map

4. Gibraltar Botanic Gardens


Six hectares of subtropical garden, laid out in 1816 so off-duty soldiers had somewhere pleasant to escape the heat. Map

  • Tip: go in mid-summer for the flowers.

Also seen: the Great Siege Tunnels and Rock Gun Battery, dug during the 1779 to 1783 siege; the Moorish Castle; and Europa Point Lighthouse at the southernmost tip.

Good to know

  • Gibraltar covers just 6.7 sq km and shares a 1.2 km land border with Spain.
  • The Strait of Gibraltar, the gateway to the Mediterranean, is only 14.3 km wide.
  • In a 2002 referendum, 98% of Gibraltarians voted against shared sovereignty with Spain.

The verdict

Would I rebook? No rush. I'll probably go back in ten years or so.
